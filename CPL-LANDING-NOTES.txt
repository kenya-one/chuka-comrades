CPL LANDING PAGE + BACKEND UPDATE

The CPL landing page remains the visual entry point. The CPL Apps Script backend is now included under:
apps-script/cpl/Code.gs

Phase 1 adds the complete Google Sheets schema and Google Drive folders for:
- League A / League B
- seasons and transfer windows
- teams, coaches, captains and players
- fixtures, lineups, referees and approved results
- match posters
- CPL Cup knockout competitions
- transfers
- collections and deletion approvals
- WhatsApp groups
- awards/votes
- news, disputes and audit logs

The next frontend step is to connect Cpl.tsx/Cpl.css to the Apps Script API and make the landing page live.
